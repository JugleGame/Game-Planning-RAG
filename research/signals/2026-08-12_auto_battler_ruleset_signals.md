## Observations Only
### Solo PvE Roguelike Auto-battler (GENRE-037)
- `Heritage` launched on Steam on 2026-08-11. The game is single-player and describes combat
  as an idle simulation driven by the attributes, skills, equipment, and life choices built
  over a run. Each year presents three Scroll choices, and the store page lists more than 160
  Scrolls, more than 140 skills, and 10 Sects. [source: Steam Store, as of 2026-08-12]
- [interpretation] `Heritage` is a new direct example for GENRE-037 because the player builds
  through repeated draft-like choices while combat resolves automatically against PvE enemies.
  Its life-course framing also shows that solo auto-battlers do not need a conventional wave
  arena.

### Familiar Physical Rules as Digital Game Foundations (ELEM-021)
- `Crown Jewels` launched on Steam on 2026-08-10 as a turn-based digital board game for two to
  four players. Each player builds a custom six-sided die, then uses movement and rule-bending
  allies to race a jewel to a crown; the page supports solo, local, and online play. [source:
  Steam Store, as of 2026-08-12]
- `Dorts: A Darts Roguelike` lists 2026-08-12 as its planned Steam release date and was still
  awaiting unlock at collection time. Its run structure starts from the familiar darts game
  501, then adds drafted upgrades, changing leg rules, short 10-to-15-minute runs, and online
  one-versus-one play. [source: Steam Store, as of 2026-08-12]
- [interpretation] `Dorts` extends familiar-ruleset appropriation beyond cards, casino games,
  dominoes, and mahjong into a physical precision sport. `Crown Jewels` is a weaker but related
  signal because it starts from dice-and-board conventions while changing the die itself.
  These two products are examples of the design direction, not evidence of commercial demand.
