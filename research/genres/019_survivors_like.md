## Components
- ELEM-022 (exponential score scaling) - The core axis in which weapon combinations multiply and cover the screen
- ELEM-031 (exaggerated visual feedback) - Visual feedback from mass kills creates pleasure
- ELEM-018 (roguelike random upgrade/path draft) - Choose one weapon/passive at each level-up
- ELEM-043 (squad simultaneous-control auto-battle) - As GAME-051 demonstrates, a variation that directs several characters at once instead of the single-character auto-battle convention

## Conventions and Expectations
- Convention: Characters attack automatically and the player only controls movement and level-up choices (the Vampire Survivors model).
- Variation: 20 Minutes Till Dawn added manual aiming to differentiate itself [source: Wikipedia, verified 2026] - pure auto-battle is conventional, but some works increase control input to differentiate.
- Variation: Yet Another Zombie Survivors (GAME-051) departs from single-character auto-battle by simultaneously controlling a squad of up to 3 [source: GAME-051 card]. ★ Re-check is needed before the official release on 2026-08-20.
