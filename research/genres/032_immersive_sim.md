## Components
- ELEM-011 (emergent systemic interaction) - the cluster's definition itself. [interpretation] This genre is the historical source of the "chemistry engine" style of design covered by ELEM-011 - System Shock, Deus Ex, Dishonored, and Prey established the philosophy of letting rules collide, which later open-world action (GENRE-005) titles inherited.
- [interpretation] Its shared identity is leaving the same problem open to multiple solutions by letting players choose combinations of stealth, combat, hacking, and environmental use instead of following a pre-scripted solution.

## Market Saturation
- Fact: none of three recent big-budget immersive sims (Prey (2017), Dishonored 2, Deus Ex: Mankind Divided) exceeded 1,000,000 copies on Steam [source: PC Gamer, "The uncertain future of games like Deus Ex and Dishonored"].
- Fact: SteamSpy estimates Prey (2017) at roughly 50-60 Steam owners, with the reported upper figure represented as 600,000 [source: ResetEra (citing SteamSpy figures)].
- Fact: Dishonored 2 has been reported with two estimates for all-platform sales, roughly 2,200,000 and roughly 2,500,000, and which is official was not confirmed [source: LEVVVEL statistics page, as verified 2026 - conflicting estimates].
- [interpretation] Big-budget new releases are increasingly difficult even to approve - Embracer Group's cancellation of a new Deus Ex project supports this [source: PC Gamer report].
- Fact: Nightdive Studios, developer of the System Shock remake, said immersive sims are a "scary investment" for publishers and are rare because their interlocking systems only come together near the end of development [source: NeoGAF/FRVR (quoting a Nightdive interview)].

## Conventions and Expectations
- [interpretation] The basic expectation is to leave at least two different solution methods open for one obstacle among stealth, combat, hacking, and environmental manipulation.
- [interpretation] The convention is to explain the world through environmental storytelling such as audio logs and notes, reducing dependence on dialogue cutscenes.
- [interpretation] Minimizing UI intervention such as minimaps and quest markers to protect "immersion" is another shared expectation - overlapping philosophically with ELEM-012 (landmark-based exploration).
