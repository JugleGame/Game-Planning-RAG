## Components
- ELEM-023 (light source / field-of-view restriction) - the identity of the cluster. Both representative titles put this device at their core,
  making the range the light reaches something to be managed as a consumable resource [source: GAME-035, GAME-036 cards].
- ELEM-013 (pixel-art graphic style) - the production condition that lets a small team realize the tone with polish.
  [interpretation] Low resolution also meshes technically with the field-of-view restriction that makes "places the light does not reach
  really invisible" [source: interpretation in the GAME-036 card].
- ELEM-016 (grimdark tone) - an axis that GAME-036 actually used together with the rest [source: GAME-036 card].

## Conventions and Expectations
- Fact: Light is handled not as staging but as a consumable resource - in GAME-035 the field of view narrows into a circle at night
  in the day/night cycle, and in GAME-036 exploration is possible only within the range the flashlight beam reaches
  [source: GAME-035, GAME-036 cards].
- [interpretation] The expectation is the consistency of "what you cannot see is actually dangerous." If the field-of-view restriction stops at a post-processing
  effect and the amount of information does not actually decrease, it can be read as a betrayal of the convention
  [source: implications in the GAME-035 card].
