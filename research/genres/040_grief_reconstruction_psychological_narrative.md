## Conventions and Expectations
- The player first receives a relationship, routine, or identity worth protecting.
- Anomalies repeat across safe and threatening contexts instead of appearing as unrelated scares. [interpretation]
- The late truth explains both the protagonist's missing knowledge and the monster or obstacle's symbolic form. [interpretation]
- The ending changes the player's relationship to a familiar action: hold, release, approach, look, or stop fleeing. [interpretation]
- Content warnings are expected when death, illness, suicide, trauma, or grief are central. GAME-058, GAME-059, and GAME-060 publish such subject warnings. [source: Steam, as of 2026-08-13]
