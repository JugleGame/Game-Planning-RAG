## Components
- ELEM-023 (light source / field-of-view restriction) - the identity of the cluster. A camera viewed from the side already hides what is off-screen, and layering light-source restriction on top narrows the visible area a second time.
- ELEM-016 (grimdark tone) - the visual language shared by the representative titles. Removing color and leaving silhouettes is close to a convention.
- ELEM-015 (stress / madness system) - not mandatory, but an option used when the horror axis is turned into a number. However, it brings along the punitive-design risk that GAME-022 demonstrated.
- [interpretation] What separates this from GENRE-009 (dark fantasy) is not the setting but the camera. A viewpoint that follows from the side makes "you don't know what is off-screen" the default.

## Conventions and Expectations
- Fact: The representative titles of this cluster are often classified as puzzle platformers rather than pure side-scrollers [source: Game Developer report, verified 2026-07].
- [interpretation] Players do not expect combat. The basic verbs are hiding, fleeing and finding the way, and if there is a moment of standing and fighting, it is staged as an exception.
- [interpretation] Atmosphere and sound are treated as entry conditions — GAME-033 kept both and still saw its evaluation fall.
