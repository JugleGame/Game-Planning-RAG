## Components
- ELEM-037 (shrinking safe zone) - the structural frame shared with GENRE-022 (battle royale). The forced-shrink structure itself is the same.
- [interpretation] The identity separating this cluster from GENRE-022 is its weapon system - the combat core is melee weapons such as swords and spears plus qinggong control (parries, dodges, and aerial movement), not firearm looting. No separate ELEM card yet covers this axis.

## Market Saturation
- Fact: GAME-049 (NARAKA: BLADEPOINT, 2021) is the cluster's largest current success, with 73% positive among about 300,000 Steam user reviews (300,000 and 1,739 reviews) [source: GAME-049 card / Steambase, as of 2026-07-31].
- Fact: Hunter's Arena: Legends (2021, Mantisco), which attempted the same trajectory, had its online service fully terminated by 2024-11-14 and was removed from Steam on 2024-04-15 [source: composite of delistedgames.com web searches, verified 2026-08].
- Fact: NetEase's open-world wuxia ARPG Where Winds Meet (global full release 2025-11-14) includes a separate battle-royale mode for up to 100 players who fight using martial arts without guns [source: composite web search (including ofzenandcomputing), verified 2026].
- [interpretation] The same publisher (NetEase) repeatedly invests in this trajectory both as a standalone title (NARAKA) and as a mode inside an open world (Where Winds Meet), while another studio's standalone attempt (Hunter's Arena: Legends) failed - an early cluster where success and failure coexist.

## Conventions and Expectations
- [interpretation] The convention appears to be setting a melee-weapon and skill set in advance instead of looting firearms, then supplementing mobility with qinggong movement such as double jumps, gliding, and wall-running (shared by NARAKA and Where Winds Meet).
- [interpretation] A wuxia setting is a required premise - even a melee-combat battle royale is not normally classified in this cluster if its setting is modern or science fiction.
