## Components
- ELEM-008 (companion): the carrier of the emotional arc. In Spiritfarer, the relationships with the spirits you must send off are themselves the game's entire structure [source: Thunder Lotus official press release, verified 2026-07].
- ELEM-012 (landmark exploration): as with the mountain summit in A Short Hike, the goal of "I want to go all the way over there" overlaps with the progression axis of the emotional arc [interpretation].
- A short, self-contained running time is the convention — A Short Hike is under 2 hours [source: Goomba Stomp review, verified 2026-07].

## Market Saturation
- Wholesome Direct, the cluster's representative showcase, grew from 25,000 viewers at its first edition in 2020 to a reach of 5,000,000 in 2025, and in 2025 60 titles were selected out of a record 1,500 submissions [source: Pixelkin / Outrun Gaming, verified 2026-07].
- Spiritfarer sold 1,000,000 units cumulatively across all platforms after its August 2020 release, and holds 95% positive out of 38,000+ Steam reviews [source: Thunder Lotus official press release / Steam, verified 2026-07].
- A Short Hike has not disclosed absolute sales. Only the developer's remark that Switch sales were "far stronger" than PC is confirmed [source: Nintendo Life, 2020-10 article].

## Conventions and Expectations
- Users expect violence-free progression, a short but self-contained emotional arc, and a warm ending [interpretation].
- A short running time is a double-edged sword: the standard reason in A Short Hike's negative reviews was "too short" [source: WayTooManyGames review, verified 2026-07].
- There is also criticism of unearned emotionality, that it "demands empathy within a short contact time" [source: Abby's Gaming Blog review, verified 2026-07].
- [interpretation] It is adjacent to GENRE-007 (cozy sim) but the axis differs: in cozy sim the endless routine loop is the core, while in this cluster a single self-contained emotional arc is the core.

## Gaps
[interpretation] ★ The combination of this cluster's structural weakness ("a shame it's short", no replay elements) with this repository's repetition-family elements (ELEM-004 repetition mechanic, ELEM-009 philosophical roguelite) — a "repeatable emotional narrative" in which relationships and partings differ each run — was not confirmed in any case during the investigation. Since GAME-009 (Hades) set the precedent of turning repetition into a reward through "death = narrative progression", a variant that puts relationships and loss on the repetition axis instead of combat appears to be a gap.
