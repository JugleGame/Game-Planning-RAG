## Components
- [interpretation] This genre's identity is production-chain design itself: mechanical logistics such as conveyors, inserters, and pipes send raw materials through processing machines in sequence. The labor force is machinery, not creatures.
- Fact: Factorio (Wube Software), Satisfactory (Coffee Stain Studios), shapez 2 (tobspr Games), and Dyson Sphere Program (Youthcat Studio) all make it the core loop for players to design chains that automatically process raw materials into finished goods through conveyors, inserters, and assemblers [source: descriptions on each game's Steam store page, verified 2026-08].
- [interpretation] The dividing question from ELEM-039 (captured-companion labor system) is whether the labor force is biological or mechanical. GENRE-030 (creature labor automation survival) assigns captured creatures by aptitude and mixes survival pressure (hunger, cold, predators) into the frame, while this genre has no creature capture and does not require survival elements - it creates fun purely through the difficulty of designing mechanical networks.

## Conventions and Expectations
- [interpretation] The convention has settled into a scale-up order: simple resource gathering → belt/pipe logistics design → multi-stage assembly lines → mass production of final goods (shared by Factorio, Satisfactory, Dyson Sphere Program, and shapez 2).
- Fact: new releases continue in 2026, including Modulus: Factory Automation (released 2026-04-02, Happy Volcano), Belts of Iron (entered early access 2026-05-28, Builderment LLC), and Sandustry (planned early access 2026-08-13, Lantto Games), so supply of new games remains active [source: composite web reporting from gamingboulevard.com·gamebrief.net, verified 2026-08].
- [interpretation] Recent releases find it difficult to stand out with the pure belt-and-inserter formula alone, so the convention has become to add one differentiator each, such as hand-built component assembly, railway logistics, or a destructible open world.

## Gaps
[interpretation] ★ The research found no hybrid between this genre (mechanical logistics automation) and GENRE-030 (replacing labor with captured creatures): the production-chain core would remain belts and machinery, while only some logistics nodes (primary gathering or simple transport) could be replaced by captured creatures. The two genres remain completely separated by different developers and market trajectories.
