## Components
- Core rule: a low-risk design that removes the failure state (game over) or makes the cost of failure close to zero. The exclusion of violence and the emphasis on relaxation are central to the genre's definition [source: Wikipedia "Cozy game" / Lostgarden "Cozy Games", verified 2026-07].
- ELEM-008 (companion): building relationships with village NPCs and neighbors often becomes the reward axis of the repeated loop [interpretation].
- ELEM-013 (pixel art): not a required condition, but a conventional pairing that combines well with low-cost, soft visuals as in GAME-019 [interpretation].
- [interpretation] The real core element of this cluster, the "no-fail low-tension loop", still has no ELEM card — it is the next element card candidate.

## Market Saturation
- The online cozy game market is projected to grow from about $1,110,000,000 in 2025 to about $1,650,000,000 in 2032, at 6.0% per year [source: QY Research, verified 2026-07]. Another survey presents 12.5% annual growth for 2025 to 2033 — the variance between institutions is large and neither is an official statistic [source: HTF Market Insights, verified 2026-07].
- There is an analysis that 62% of new releases follow a similar loop centered on farming/home decorating, so demand growth and content homogenization are proceeding at the same time [source: Intel Market Research, verified 2026-07].
- Fatigue with "yet another farming sim + X" clones (Coral Island, Sun Haven, etc. are listed) is raised repeatedly in the community [source: Crossplay (Medium) contribution, verified 2026-07].
- The cluster's single representative work GAME-019 (Stardew Valley) alone accounts for 50,000,000+ units [source: GAME-019 card].
- July 2026 was reported as a dense stretch of cozy game releases - publishers judged summer to be the right time for a genre of "long, leisurely play sessions", cat-themed titles in particular were unusually dense, and the analysis holds that cat content is consistently superior in engagement and social media sharing [source: GAMES.GG "July 2026 Cozy Game Launches", verified 2026-07 / digest 2026-07-25].
- Steam cozy new releases in 2026 are tallied at 43 titles as of July - this is by an aggregation site's standard and is not an official statistic [source: Cozy Game Reviews, verified 2026-07 / digest 2026-07-25].
- [interpretation] On top of the existing observation that "62% of new releases use the farming/decorating loop", a concentration at the theme level (cats) has also been confirmed, so homogenization appears to be proceeding at the subject-matter layer as well as in loop structure.

## Conventions and Expectations
- Users expect no game over, no time pressure, and self-paced progression as defaults [source: Wikipedia "Cozy game" / Game Developer contribution "The Calming Effect of Low Stakes Games", verified 2026-07].
- Abundance of resources, safe spaces, and soft visuals and sound are the conventions [source: Lostgarden "Cozy Games", verified 2026-07].
- [interpretation] It overlaps with GENRE-006 (pixel 2D sandbox) in representative works (GAME-019), but the cluster's axis differs: GENRE-006 is defined by visuals and structure (free exploration in pixel 2D), while this cluster is defined by the tension curve (no failure, low stress).

## Gaps
[interpretation] ★ If 62% of new releases are crowded into the farming/decorating loop, there is room for differentiation just by changing the loop's material. In particular, the combination with ELEM-005 (AI integration) and ELEM-006 (AI interrogation) — a cozy game whose core loop is low-tension everyday conversation with an AI companion — was not confirmed in any case during the investigation. Turning around the fact that the retention failure case of GENRE-003 (AI-native) (GAME-010) was a high-tension design, there is a possibility that a no-fail low-tension structure is a better vessel for repeated use of AI interaction.
