## Definition
An approach that collects anime-art-style characters through gacha. It is not merely that combat stats differ; each character comes with voice-acted lines, a short story of their own and dedicated costumes, so the core is making players pull because they like a character, not because it is strong.

## Success Cases
- GAME-046 (Wuthering Waves) - Steam reviews 88/100 (52,627 reviews, Very Positive), and on the day of the 3.0 update it set a new self-record for Steam concurrency at 40,721 players [source: Steambase/X (ByteStix) aggregation, verified 2026-07].
- GAME-047 (Zenless Zone Zero) - Steam reviews 87/100 (8,194 reviews, Very Positive), with a peak concurrency of 15,200 players on its Steam release day of 2026-06-17 [source: Steambase/comicbook.com, as of 2026-06].
- GAME-024 (Genshin Impact) - Surpassed $10,000,000,000 in cumulative revenue as of the end of 2025, the fastest such achievement among mobile games ever [source: STG Research aggregation, as of the end of 2025].

## Failure Cases
- GAME-048 (NIKKE) - On mobile it recorded the top subculture revenue rank for 3 consecutive months as of May 2026 [source: Inven/Gamerscroll ranking coverage, as of 2026-05], but the Steam version was effectively neglected, at a level of 6 total reviews and 1 concurrent player [source: Steambase, verified 2026-07].
  Failure point: character collection itself was a hit, but the PC platform (Steam) entry strategy failed separately - it shows that the success or failure of an element and of a platform strategy can be separated.

## User Reaction Summary
- Preferred: [interpretation] investment in voice acting and narrative that "makes characters feel like living people" is frequently cited as the core driver of sustained long-term spending.
- Disliked: [interpretation] because the structure keeps releasing new characters, "power creep / character fatigue" - in which users attached to existing characters feel alienated by the next banner - appears as a recurring complaint.

## Synergy
- Genre anchor: GENRE-025 (Subculture Game) - this cluster names this element as a component.
- Good: ELEM-017 (Gacha Rates & Pity System) - because the characters themselves are the core of the appeal, combining them with a probability device makes the desire to "own this character" translate directly into spending.

## Risks
- [interpretation] Because appeal is concentrated in individual characters, a controversy over a single popular character (voice actor issues, design plagiarism allegations, etc.) can directly affect the game's overall revenue.
- [interpretation] The gap in performance across platforms is large (the NIKKE case) - porting a mobile-centric design to Steam as-is does not automatically produce a hit.
