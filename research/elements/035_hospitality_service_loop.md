## Definition
A service-business (tavern, cafe, etc.) simulation approach that keeps repeating the short interaction of hearing what a customer wants when they arrive, picking ingredients, making something and handing it over. The business itself becomes an excuse to keep a conversation going with the customer.

## Success Cases
- Long Live My Lady! 🍻 Tavern Simulator (2026, Sudo Eat Cake Games) - A pixel-art tavern simulation in which a gnome stands in for the sleeping innkeeper, serving customers while brewing drinks and cooking food to sell. Since its Steam release (2026-05-01), 95% positive out of 22 reviews as of the verification point [source: Steam store page (store.steampowered.com/app/3593040), as of 2026]. (At other verification points it was also tallied as 94% positive out of 17-18 reviews, so the figures diverge - conflict: true; this appears to stem from the review count rising in real time.)
- VA-11 Hall-A: Cyberpunk Bartender Action (2016, Sukeban Games) - A structure where you become a bartender, mix and hand over drinks, and listen to customers' stories, so the act of service itself is the device that opens conversation. 96% positive out of 12,020 reviews ("Overwhelmingly Positive"), with an estimated 500,000-1,000,000 Steam owners [source: Steam store page, SteamSpy].
- Coffee Talk (2020, Toge Productions) - A structure that looks into customers' stories through the simple service action of picking and combining three ingredients to serve a drink [source: composite of game review outlet coverage (Bits & Pieces, VideoGameGeek)].

## User Reaction Summary
- Preferred: praise for the structure of "the conversation with the customer is the real purpose and the service is the excuse" - reactions such as "extremely comfy" and meaningful conversation (VA-11 Hall-A) [source: Steam store page review summary].
- Preferred: the assessment that a character pairing like gnome-and-princess is appealing on personality alone, without a high-stakes fantasy narrative [source: IndieBunny feature article].

## Synergy
- ELEM-013 (Pixel Art Style) - A case where Long Live My Lady implemented a hospitality loop while choosing pixel art for a low-cost pipeline [source: Steam store page].
- GENRE-007 (Cozy Sim) - [interpretation] A hospitality loop that repeats at low tension without fail states overlaps exactly with the core condition of the cozy sim cluster (low-stress repetition).

## Risks
- [interpretation] If the service action itself (ingredient combination and serving) is too simple, repetition fatigue can set in within a few hours - both VA-11 Hall-A and Coffee Talk chose a structure that compensates for this simplicity with dialogue and narrative.
- [interpretation] Because the production cost of dialogue and narrative content exceeds that of the hospitality mechanic itself, long-term retention can be difficult if content consumption speed outpaces development speed.
