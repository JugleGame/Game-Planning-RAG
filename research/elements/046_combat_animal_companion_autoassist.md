## Definition
The player directly controls only one character, but an animal companion nearby attacks enemies or performs support actions during combat without separate commands. It is like two people on a team where one is controlled by the player and the other fights on its own beside them. The key is that the companion is not merely reacting through dialogue but handles a substantial combat offense and support role.

## Success Cases
- Beast of Reincarnation (2026, Game Freak) - An action RPG consisting of a single character (Emma) and an animal companion dog (Koo). When directed by the player, Koo attacks enemies or activates the special turn-based-menu skill "Bloom Arts" with a separate button, participating materially in combat [source: PlayStation Blog hands-on, as of 2026-08-03]. Reviews called the combat itself "appealing combat centered on parries" (PlayStation Blog) and "captivating combat" (Game Informer). Metacritic aggregate 74 (35 critic reviews; PC 72 / PS5 73) [source: Metacritic aggregation, as of 2026-08].

## User Reaction Summary
- Preference: Affection for the combat and Koo as a character - "the most lovable mutant puppy" [source: PlayStation Blog hands-on, as of 2026-08-03]
- Aversion: Repeated evaluations that the game's overall journey (world and structure) is dull [source: VGC review title ("disappointingly dull journey"), Game Informer review title ("Boring World"), as of 2026-08] - however, this is dissatisfaction with world design rather than the companion-combat mechanic itself.

## Synergy
- Distinction[interpretation]: ELEM-008 (companion / playable-together character) - ELEM-008 covers companions that react narratively beside the player (the Party Animals and CielChan cases), and whether they participate in combat is not its core. This element is a narrower subtype in which the companion takes on combat offense and support automatically without separate control, so it is distinguished from ELEM-008.

## Risks
- [interpretation] Because the companion autonomously decides attacks and support, it may feel obstructive if its aim or timing conflicts with player intent. However, no such complaint about this game was confirmed in the investigation.
- [interpretation] If the companion's combat contribution becomes greater than that of the player-controlled character, confusion may arise about what kind of game the player is controlling.
