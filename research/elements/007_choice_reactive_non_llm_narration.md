## Definition
Although it appears that the story changes depending on the choices the player makes, it is actually a way of showing one of several branches of the story that the developer has written in advance. This is a common technique that has been used for a long time, and this card was created using Detroit: Become Human and Disco Elysium as representative examples [source: Eneba hub article].

## Success Cases
- Detroit: Become Human - 2024 As of the end of 2024, it sold over 11,000,000 copies worldwide, according to a secondary aggregator article (not an official announcement) [source: Eneba hub article (secondary aggregator, not an official Sony/Quantic Dream statement)].
- GAME-041 (Baldur's Gate 3) - A case of creating the impression that "choices really change the outcome" with extensive pre-written branching dialogue and reactive events. With more than 20,000,000 cumulative copies in 2 years and 870,000 + 5,000 concurrent users, it ranked among the top 10 in Steam history and won Game of the Year at major awards ceremonies - this factor is the basis for showing that it is possible to achieve top-level commercial performance even without LLM [source: GAME-041 card].
- Disco Elysium - Known to have over 1,000,000 words of dialogue, cited as a prime example of the scale a responsive non-LLM narrative can reach [source: Evergreen Review].

## Failure Cases
- A design critique essay argues that traditional branching dialogue trees (non-LLM methods) cannot be truly "responsive" because all the text is already written before the player's first choice [source: Evergreen Review]. Even though Disco Elysium has over 1,000,000 words of dialogue, most major events cannot be avoided when replaying [source: Evergreen Review].

## User Reaction Summary
- Preference: Solid branching narrative and character writing - Detroit: Become Human and Disco Elysium are mentioned as examples [source: Summary of user reactions from survey data]
- Dislike: Illusion of choice (Criticism of the fact that it is a fixed branching dialogue tree) [source: Summary of user reactions from survey data]

## Synergy
- Good[interpretation]: ELEM-008 (companion / co-playable character) - The sense of "my choice is remembered" is strongest when pre-written branches are retrieved through the reactions of fellow characters. GAME-041 is an example of a branching narrative with colleagues and cooperation.
- Contrast [interpretation]: ELEM-005 (AI integration) - The same goal (narrative that responds to player choices) is achieved through script and real-time generation. The cost structure and verification difficulty are opposite, so choosing one directly determines the scale of production.
- Genre anchor: GENRE-021 (branching visual novel), GENRE-033 (CRPG) - Two groups point out this element as a component.

## Risks
- [interpretation] Although it may seem like there are many paths, it is actually limited pre-made content, so there is a risk that the meaning of the choice will fade the more you replay it (based on Evergreen Review criticism).
- [interpretation] The cost and time involved in pre-producing large volumes (e.g. more than 100 1,000,000 words) is very high.
