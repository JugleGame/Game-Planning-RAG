## Definition
A structure in which you receive money at the end of each round according to how well you did (win/loss, kills), and buy weapons and equipment with that money before the next round starts. If you fail to earn in the previous round, you start the next round poor as well, producing a snowball effect of continually falling behind.

## Success Cases
- GAME-042 (Counter-Strike 2) - 85% positive reviews (9.7M+ reviews), with a 30-day average concurrency of about 920,000 players as of 2026-07 [source: Steambase, verified 2026-07]. The core identity of a franchise that has continued for over 20 years.
