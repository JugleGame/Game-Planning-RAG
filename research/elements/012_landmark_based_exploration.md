## Definition
Instead of following arrows or icons on a map, players see landmarks such as mountains, towers, or castles from far away and want to go there, finding their own route. The destination is not announced; it is made conspicuous and becomes an object of curiosity.

## Success Cases
- GAME-016 (Elden Ring) - Guided players with landmarks such as castles, mist, and towers instead of icons. PC Metacritic score 95 [source: GAME-016 card].
- GAME-014 (Breath of the Wild) - Towers expand the map, while visible terrain lets players infer destinations instead of using quest markers. Sold 34,320,000 copies cumulatively [source: GAME-014 card].
- GAME-015 (Tears of the Kingdom) - Connected the surface, sky, and underground layers through landmarks so players could find routes without markers. Reached 10,000,000 copies in three days, the series’ best launch [source: GAME-015 card].

## Failure Cases
- GAME-017 (Assassin's Creed Valhalla) - After tower synchronization, the map was covered in icons, leading to criticism that “discovery” became a “collection checklist.” 150 hours for completion according to HowLongToBeat [source: GAME-017 card].
  Failure point: The practical destinations became the “list of icons revealed by synchronization,” not the landmarks themselves.

## User Reaction Summary
- Preference: Inferring “what might be beyond the hill” and walking there [source: ScreenRant analysis, re-cited by GAME-016 card]
- Dislike: Fatigue with icon-covered maps - “Ubisoft map” is used as shorthand for icon excess [source: industry criticism roundup (editorialge.com), confirmed 2026-07]
- Mixed: “People criticize hand-holding but actually want help” - Elden Ring guide content has overwhelmingly higher views than guides for other games [source: TheGamer, published 2022-03-15]

## Synergy
- Good: ELEM-011 (Emergent Systemic Interaction) - The route must offer experiments so reaching a landmark feels like exploration, not a round trip.
- Good: ELEM-034 (Exploration Photography Goal Verb) - [interpretation] Combining arrival with the goal of leaving photographic evidence makes the reward a shareable result rather than a completion mark.
- Conflict: Genres requiring strong linear progression (story-mission games with a fixed order) - Removing markers risks making players lose the main route [interpretation]
- Genre anchors: GENRE-005 (Open World), GENRE-008 (Emotional Narrative Adventure), GENRE-017 (Metroidvania) - All three clusters identify this element as a component. GENRE-008 uses the goal of wanting to reach “that place” as part of the emotional arc [source: GENRE-008 card].

## Risks
- [interpretation] Players clearly divide between those who find wandering for a lost goal fun and those who find it unfriendly - it suits difficult action genres but is risky for casual-oriented genres.
- [interpretation] Landmark placement requires much more refined level-design skill - removing markers is terrain-design work, not UI work.
