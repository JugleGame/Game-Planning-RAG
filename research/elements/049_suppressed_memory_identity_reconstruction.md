## Definition
The main character does not know, or refuses to know, an important part of the past. The player finds small pieces such as places, habits, documents, photographs, or sounds. When the pieces join, earlier strange events gain a clear meaning.

## Success Cases
- GAME-058 (OMORI): the official premise makes navigation through the vibrant and mundane a route toward uncovering a forgotten past; it sold 1,000,000 copies. [source: Steam, as of 2026-08-13] [source: OMOCAT official blog, as of 2022-12-31]
- GAME-059 (Before Your Eyes): the Ferryman forces the protagonist beneath selected life memories into repressed material and a hidden truth; the game won a BAFTA. [source: Steam, as of 2026-08-13] [source: BAFTA, as of 2022-04-07]
- GAME-060 (SOMA): terminals, secret documents, and encounters uncover an identity-centered truth while the game maintains Overwhelmingly Positive Steam reception. [source: Steam, as of 2026-08-13]

## Risks
- [interpretation] Amnesia becomes a writer shortcut if the protagonist forgets only information convenient to the twist.
- [interpretation] The reveal fails fairness when earlier clues have only one meaning before the ending and an unrelated meaning after it.
- [interpretation] A collectible list is not reconstruction by itself; the player must use the pieces to revise a belief or action.
