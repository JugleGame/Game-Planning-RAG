## Definition
Grimdark tone is an atmosphere in which the world is desperate and people are morally gray. An extraction-run structure is a rule in which what is gained in a round becomes truly yours only if you carry it out alive. Combined, the tension of risking yourself, securing your load, and escaping builds simultaneously at the system level (the rule that things can be lost) and the worldbuilding level (a world with no hope to begin with). Death feels painful for two reasons at once.

## Success Cases
- Eldegarde (Notorious Studios, officially released 2026-01) - An extraction RPG in which players use fantasy classes such as warrior, rogue, and mage to fight monsters, knights, and other players, find loot in forests, wetlands, and underground tombs, and escape safely [source: TechPowerUp, as of 2026-01-21].

## Failure Cases
- Mistfall Hunter (Bellring Games, officially released 2026-07-29) - About 430,000 people participated in the open beta (Next Fest) and it reached third in Steam sales after release, but reviews settled at "Mixed" after the official launch, with only 67% positive among 3,548 reviews [source: Inven Global report / Steam, verified 2026-08]. [interpretation] The split between initial interest (open-beta participation and sales rank) and actual settlement evaluation (review ratio) shows that this combination can create buzz without automatically producing long-term satisfaction.

## User Reaction Summary
- Preference: The comparison "Tarkov with a sword instead of a gun" appears repeatedly, evaluating it as repackaging familiar extraction-shooter tension for fans in a fantasy tone [source: Notebookcheck report, verified 2026-08].
- Aversion: A Steam discussion thread expressed fatigue with "another dark fantasy extraction game" - new games with the same combination appeared in a short period, signaling genre saturation [source: Steam community discussion "Another Dark Fantasy ARPG Extraction game?", verified 2026-08].

## Synergy
- Essential: ELEM-016 (grimdark tone) - The weight of the world. Without this tone, extraction failure is merely resource loss.
- Essential: ELEM-027 (extraction-run structure) - The body of the rules. If the tone is grimdark but death has no systemic cost, the combination does not hold.
- Genres spanning this combination: GENRE-009 (dark fantasy) and GENRE-011 (looter shooter) each partially contain its materials (grimdark worldbuilding and risk-reward farming), but no genre card yet defines the combination itself, where rewards depend on successful extraction.

## Risks
- [interpretation] New games using the same combination have clustered in a short period (including Eldegarde and Mistfall Hunter), and saturation signals already appear. A late entrant may remain "Mixed" without differentiation.
- [interpretation] PvPvE (being able to die to other players) is often used with it, so grimdark despair may be felt as frustration with other players. The intended weight is worldbuilding, but the experienced feeling may be PvP unpleasantness.
