## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
Free to play (inheriting CS:GO, transitioned 2023-09). More than 8,500,000 reviews were 85% positive ("Very Positive"), with about 920,000 average concurrent players over 30 days as of 2026-07 [source: Steambase, verified 2026-07].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-038 (round-based economy system) - directly inherited the 20-year franchise identity in which weapon purchases and fund management each round determine victory or defeat.

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: 85% positive reviews, 9.7M+ reviews [source: Steambase, verified 2026-07].
- Fact: At launch (2023-09), the subtick system was announced as replacing 128 ticks, but matchmaking actually remained at 64 ticks; the community objected to judgment inconsistency and reduced content compared with CS:GO (War Games and Danger Zone removed) [source: Forbes/CSMARKET/Sportskeeda coverage, as of 2023-09].
- [interpretation] Twenty years of franchise momentum and the intrinsic polish of the economy system offset the initial backlash and preserved long-term metrics.

## Implications for Our Project
[interpretation] As long as the core identity (round-based economy) is not disturbed, temporary content loss during a technology migration (such as an engine replacement) may not be fatal to long-term metrics—but elements directly tied to the core loop's "felt fairness," such as server ticks, must be treated as exceptions.
