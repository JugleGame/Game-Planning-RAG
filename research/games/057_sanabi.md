## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
SANABI is a dystopian action platformer built around a prosthetic chain-hook arm and a mystery involving a retired veteran, his daughter, and the megacity he enters for one last operation. Steam lists 41,149 purchaser reviews across all languages as Overwhelmingly Positive and 4,678 English reviews with 95% positive sentiment. [source: Steam, as of 2026-08-13]

## Elements Used
- ELEM-050 (Core Verb as Narrative Metaphor): the chain-hook arm is both the repeated traversal/combat verb and the protagonist's signature identity. [source: Steam SANABI store description, as of 2026-08-13]
- [interpretation] The action loop carries the player through long mystery setup, while companion dialogue and flashback-shaped information give that movement an emotional destination.

## Success/Failure Drivers
- SANABI won Best Action Adventure Game at the 2024 Indie Game Development Awards; the publisher highlighted both its chain-hook action and the father's emotional journey. [source: NEOWIZ press release, as of 2024-07-31]
- Steam users tag the game as both Story Rich and Action, while the current review volume remains Overwhelmingly Positive. [source: Steam, as of 2026-08-13]
- [interpretation] Its reusable lesson is not merely “add a twist.” The market signal supports a package in which the core movement is satisfying before the player understands the full story.

## Implications for Our Project
- [interpretation] Keep the moment-to-moment verb enjoyable during setup chapters; emotional exposition should change why the player acts, not replace play for long stretches.
- [interpretation] Seed the reveal through a companion relationship and recurring physical details, so the ending reorganizes evidence the player already handled.
- [interpretation] Give the final interaction a mechanical echo of the core verb, allowing the climax to be performed rather than only watched.
