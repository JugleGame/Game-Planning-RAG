## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
After its PC release in 2023-08, it recorded about $600,000,000 + $57,000,000 in cumulative Steam revenue and became the year's best-selling new Steam release [source: gamepressure.com coverage, as of 2024-01]. Cumulative sales exceeded 20,000,000 units over the following two years [source: Outlook Respawn / ResetEra statistics coverage, as of 2025]. Its peak concurrent player count of 870,000 + 5,000 placed it in Steam's all-time concurrent top 10 [source: industry statistics, verified 2026]. It won Game of the Year at The Game Awards, the Golden Joystick, and the Steam Awards [source: awards coverage roundup, as of 2023-12].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-007 (choice-responsive non-LLM narrative) - creates the impression that "choices truly change outcomes" through extensive prewritten branching dialogue and responsive events.

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: During the Early Access period (beginning 2020-10), it refined Act 1 with community feedback for nearly three years before full release [source: Larian development disclosures, industry coverage roundup].
- [interpretation] It faithfully reproduced tabletop D&D rules while supporting full cooperative play, attracting both the existing CRPG audience and a new cooperative-player audience.

## Implications for Our Project
Extensive branching narrative requires significant development time and cost, but validating early content over a long period through Early Access before full release can reduce risk. This is a useful example of cooperative-play support greatly widening the potential buyer base of a single-player CRPG genre.
