## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
Sequel to Breath of the Wild. Sold 10,000,000 units worldwide in just 3 days after release, the best launch performance in the series [source: Game World Observer, as of 2023-05-17]. Later grew to about 18,510,000 units [source: TweakTown (citing Nintendo earnings announcement), as of the May 2023 quarter]. Metacritic 96 (101 reviews) [source: Metacritic, verified 2026-07].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-011 (Emergent System Interaction) - Ultrahand lets players stick objects together to build vehicles and devices themselves, expanding the previous game's "elemental reactions" all the way to "structure assembly" [source: article summarizing developer interviews (Merry-Go-Round Magazine), verified 2026-07].
- ELEM-012 (Landmark-Based Exploration) - Connects the three layers of surface, sky, and depths through terrain features so players find their own routes without markers [interpretation].

## Success/Failure Drivers
- [interpretation] Physics rules where "whatever you combine makes some kind of sense" made puzzles with more than one right answer possible, and this is the core of what kept the game in conversation without requiring repeat purchases.
- The New Yorker assessed that the game "encourages experimentation and improvisation instead of pushing you down a set path" [source: The New Yorker review, as re-cited in an editorialge.com article, verified 2026-07].

## Implications for Our Project
[interpretation] Even with the same rule set, raising the "degree of freedom in combination" by one step (elemental reactions → structure assembly) can produce entirely new emergence while reusing an already validated world and assets.
