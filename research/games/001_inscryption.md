## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
A blend of deckbuilding + escape room + psychological horror. 1,000,000 units within one month of release [source: Devolver announcement, as of 2022-01],
cumulative 1,460,000 units [source: Devolver earnings announcement, as of 2022-09].
Of over 100,000 Steam reviews, 96% positive, "Overwhelmingly Positive" [source: Steam, verified 2026-07-14].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-003 (Forced Wall Breaking) - a frame structure in which a single-act card game expands into save files and reality
- ELEM-001 (Teaser-Based) - locked rooms and unreadable cards keep hinting at "the next floor"

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: sales velocity was abnormally fast relative to marketing spend - 1,000,000 units in the first month [source: Devolver, 2022-01]
- [interpretation] The meta twist created a "no-spoilers, just play it" culture, making word of mouth itself the marketing
- [interpretation] However, this was only possible because the card loop before the twist was already fun on its own - the twist alone could not have carried it

## Implications for Our Project
The twist (forced wall) is a diffusion device; the core loop is a retention device. Their roles differ.
The narrator system likewise must be layered on top of a "loop that is fun even with the AI turned off".
