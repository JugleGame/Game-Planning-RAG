## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
OMORI moves through a strange world of colorful friends and foes, alternating the vibrant and mundane while the player uncovers a forgotten past. It has sold 1,000,000 copies and Steam lists 79,329 purchaser reviews across all languages as Overwhelmingly Positive, with 97% positive sentiment among 54,079 English reviews. ## Implications for Our Project
- [interpretation] Let the player perform ordinary friendship activities before the first major horror rupture; later fear then threatens a relationship the player helped build.
- [interpretation] Repeat a small set of anomalies across safe and nightmare spaces so recovered memory joins two apparently separate layers.
- [interpretation] Protect the late truth from premature exposition, but make every major clue legible in hindsight.
