## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
- Fact: Developed by 24 Entertainment and operated by NetEase Games, it is a wuxia action battle royale for up to 60 players [source: Wikipedia/Steam page, verified 2026-07].
- Fact: 73% positive ("Mostly Positive") among approximately 300,000 and 1,739 Steam user reviews [source: Steambase figures, as of 2026-07-31].
- Fact: Although daily active users were reported to exceed 1,000,000, Steam concurrent users were around 40,000 and 1,111 [source: industry-report compilation/steam-stats.com, verified 2026].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-025 (on-device SLM real-time voice-conversation NPC) - An NVIDIA ACE-based on-device AI teammate, 'Viper,' was added to the mobile/PC version of NARAKA: BLADEPOINT on 2026-03-27. ACE speech recognition understands the player's voice commands, and a small language model decides and autonomously performs actions such as tagging enemies and reporting the battle situation [source: NVIDIA GeForce News/wccftech, verified 2026].

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: Viper reportedly actively handles combat and farming by joining the party, fighting alongside the player, finding needed items, changing equipment, and suggesting skill choices [source: NVIDIA GeForce News, verified 2026].
- Fact: Alongside GAME-011 (inZOI), this feature is cited as a representative case of "NVIDIA ACE being included in an actual release build beyond a demo" [source: NVIDIA GeForce News/PCGamer, verified 2026-07].
- [interpretation] Unlike inZOI, whose core is text and inner-life simulation itself, NARAKA added an AI teammate as a support feature on top of an already validated PvP battle royale - its adoption path differs from a structure like GENRE-003 (AI-native game), where "the game does not work without AI."
