## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
Free to play. Reviews were 81% positive (2.7M+ reviews); as of 2026-07, average 30-day concurrent players were about 420,000–560,000, and it ranked second on Steam by DAU [source: Steambase/activeplayer.io, verified 2026-07].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-036 (hero pick/ban draft) - a hero pool of more than 100 and the ban/pick phase form a core strategic layer before actual combat.

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: It recorded an all-time peak concurrent count of 1,290,000 in 2016-03, stayed near the top for more than 10 years, and recorded 960,000 concurrent players again during The International in 2025 [source: activeplayer.io and others, roundup verified 2026-07].
- [interpretation] The free-to-play policy plus a completely free hero roster (spending does not affect combat power) appears central to survival on a 10-year scale.

## Implications for Our Project
[interpretation] Monetization that does not create power imbalance (cosmetics-only) is favorable for long-term survival—in a genre where "the choice itself is the match," as in a draft, selling access to widen those choices would destroy the identity.
