## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
- Fact: Four Quarters developed it and Devolver Digital published it for PC (Steam) on 2021-03-04 [source: PC Gamer report, as of 2021-02].
- Fact: It sold 500,000 copies in its first week alone and surpassed 1,000,000 cumulative copies in December (month 12) 2021 [source: TechRaptor/Gfinity Esports reports, as of 2021].
- Fact: It recorded more than 51,000 Steam concurrent users at launch [source: Gfinity Esports report, as of 2021].
- Fact: Steam overall reviews are "Very Positive," reported as either 91% positive among 16,074 total reviews or 93% positive among approximately 36,373, depending on the aggregation time and source [source: Steambase figures, verified 2026-08, conflict: true].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-004 (repetition mechanic) - The character only walks a pre-laid loop path automatically, while the player balances risk and reward by deciding where to place tiles (enemy spawners and resource terrain) on it [source: Windows Central developer interview, verified 2026-08].
- GENRE-037 (solo PvE roguelike autobattler) - It is a seminal case in which combat proceeds fully automatically and the player intervenes only in loop-path design and upgrade selection [source: Windows Central interview/Medium design analysis, verified 2026-08].

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: The development team (Four Quarters) said it commercialized only the idea of "one hero going around a loop" from a failed game-jam (Ludum Dare) prototype, adding the story later to support the gameplay [source: Windows Central interview, verified 2026-08].
- [interpretation] Narrowing the choice set to deciding only "what to place" on top of fully automatic combat appears to have lowered the entry barrier, leading to an unusual first-week sale of 500,000 copies for an indie release.
