## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
An interactive thriller about a man trapped in a 12-minute loop (with a famous voice cast). Overall Steam rating
"Mixed" - of about 3,500 English reviews, 68% positive, out of some 8,000 in total
[source: Steam store, verified 2026-07]. The Metacritic user score is in the 5-point range [source:
ScreenRant aggregate, 2021-08].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-004 (Repetition Mechanic) - evidence on the failure side. The loop exists, but what is refreshed each loop (new information, new lines, new rewards) is insufficient, and there was no means to skip.

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: expectations before release were high thanks to the concept and casting - it succeeded in securing buzz
- Fact: complaint keywords converge on "an interesting mystery degenerating into a slog", "re-listening to the same lines over and over", and "having to redo the whole loop when stuck" [source: Engadget/Metacritic user reviews, 2021-08]
- [interpretation] The divisiveness over the twist (incest subject matter) compounded this, deepening the split in evaluation - loop fatigue was the base deduction, the twist an additional one.

## Implications for Our Project
The representative demonstration of the ELEM-004 risk ("repetition by itself is not content").
Checklist when designing a loop game: (1) elements refreshed each loop (2) means of skipping repeated segments (3) dialogue variation per iteration.
