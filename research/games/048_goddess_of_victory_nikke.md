## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
As of May 2026, it ranked first globally in subculture-game revenue on mobile (for three consecutive months) [source: GamerScroll rankings report, as of 2026-05]. By contrast, the Steam version (released 2023-02-15) has only 6 total reviews and about 1 concurrent user [source: Steambase, verified 2026-07]. On mobile (Google Play), it has 5,000,000+ downloads and a 4.5 rating [source: Statista/Sportskeeda compilation, verified 2026-07].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-040 (anime-style character collection) - It combines third-person shooter combat with character-specific narrative and voice acting.
- ELEM-017 (gacha odds & pity system) - Character pulls are the core revenue source.

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: It maintained first place in mobile revenue for three consecutive months [source: Inven/GamerScroll, as of 2026-05].
- Fact: The Steam version of the same game has only 6 reviews [source: Steambase, verified 2026-07].
- [interpretation] The success of the elements themselves (character collection and gacha) is separate from success on a particular platform - a subculture game designed and marketed mobile-first does not automatically transfer by simply being placed on Steam.

## Implications for Our Project
[interpretation] When porting to another platform, it is risky to assume that "the validated core loop will make it succeed" - without new content and marketing like GAME-047 (ZZZ), the port itself can be meaningless as in this card.
