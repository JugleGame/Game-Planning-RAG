## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: It separates the item economy through a binary faction choice (Circle of Fortune - solo farming only / Merchant's Guild - trading only) [source: official roadmap/build-guide report, verified 2026].
- [interpretation] The explosive launch success (162,000 concurrent users and surpassing Elden Ring) appears to have resulted from 1.0 arriving after a community accumulated during long Early Access (2020~2024); like the GENRE-034 card's "interest drops between seasons" pattern, its recent-30-day positive review rate (63%) is below the overall positive rate (77%), suggesting fluctuation with the seasonal cycle.
