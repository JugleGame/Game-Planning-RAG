## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
Before Your Eyes is a first-person narrative adventure about a soul's journey into the afterlife, with real-life blinks controlling movement through joyous and heartbreaking memories. Steam lists 20,875 purchaser reviews across all languages as Overwhelmingly Positive and 15,368 English reviews with 98% positive sentiment. [source: Steam, as of 2026-08-13]

## Elements Used
- ELEM-048 (Mundane Bonding / Horror Contrast): charming memories of family and first love are placed beside illness, death, grief, and regret. [source: Steam Before Your Eyes store description, as of 2026-08-13]
- ELEM-049 (Suppressed Memory and Identity Reconstruction): the Ferryman pushes the protagonist beneath curated life memories into repressed material that reveals the hidden truth. [source: Steam Before Your Eyes store description, as of 2026-08-13]
- ELEM-050 (Core Verb as Narrative Metaphor): an ordinary blink both advances time and makes the player lose moments they wanted to keep. [source: GoodbyeWorld Games developer FAQ, as of 2021-04-08]

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Before Your Eyes won the 2022 BAFTA Game Beyond Entertainment award. [source: BAFTA Games Awards winners announcement, as of 2022-04-07]
- Its Steam reception is Overwhelmingly Positive across 20,875 purchaser reviews. [source: Steam, as of 2026-08-13]
- Eye-detection failures on Windows required an official patch, showing that a bespoke input can interrupt the narrative when its sensing layer is unreliable. [source: GoodbyeWorld Games Steam announcement, as of 2022-10-18]

## Implications for Our Project
- [interpretation] A final refusal to flee can work like the blink: one simple input gains emotional meaning because earlier chapters trained a different expectation.
- [interpretation] Preserve an accessible standard-input path; a narrative should not become unavailable when optional sensing or timing fails.
- [interpretation] The identity truth should answer why the player has been moving through memories, not exist only as a surprise label.
