## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
- Fact: Developed by Awesome Games Studio, it launched in Steam Early Access on 2023-07-13 [source: Steambase, verified 2026-08].
- Fact: Its full release (ending Early Access) is scheduled for 2026-08-20, launching simultaneously on Steam, GOG, PlayStation, and Xbox; Nintendo Switch support will follow [source: Games Press/Saving Content report, as of 2026-07-24].
- Fact: It sold more than 600,000 copies during Early Access, with 91% positive ("Very Positive") among more than 10,000 and 3,000 Steam reviews [source: report compilation, verified 2026-07].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-022 (exponential score scaling) - Like the survivor-like archetype (GAME-037), it shares late-game scaling in which weapon and passive combinations grow multiplicatively [interpretation].
- ELEM-018 (roguelike random upgrade/path draft) - It shares a draft structure where the player chooses one weapon or ability on level-up [interpretation].
- GENRE-019 (survivor-like) - It departs from the genre convention of single-character auto-combat by introducing a variation in which one player simultaneously runs a squad of up to 3 [source: gamespress.com/thexboxhub.com report, verified 2026].
- ELEM-043 (squad simultaneous-control auto-combat) - This element card covers the variation above, for which this game is a representative practical example.

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: The squad system and meaningful meta-progression are cited as strengths, while rescues and item pickups throughout the maps create incentives to move [source: rogueliker.com review, verified 2026].
- Fact: The three-soldier squad and varied combinations are praised as distinguishing it from "another Vampire Survivors clone" [source: review compilation, verified 2026].
- [interpretation] Replacing the genre convention of single-character auto-combat (GENRE-019) with simultaneous squad control appears to have contributed to the high 91% positive rating.
