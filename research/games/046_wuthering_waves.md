## Summary and Sales/Review Metrics
Steam reviews 88/100 (52,627 reviews, Very Positive) [source: Steambase, verified 2026-07]. After its Steam launch
(2025-04-28), its first peak concurrent users were 10,888, rising to 40,721 on the day of the 3.0 update and setting a new Steam record
[source: WNHub/X(ByteStix), verified 2026-07].

## Elements Used
- ELEM-040 (anime-style character collection) - Voice acting, narrative, and promotional videos create buzz for each new character.
- ELEM-017 (gacha odds & pity system) - Rotating character and weapon banners are a monetization pillar.

## Success/Failure Drivers
- Fact: Reports indicated that daily active users were still declining even one year after the original May 2024 release [source: gamepressure.com, as of 2025].
- Fact: Steam-only concurrent users reached a new record when the Steam launch coincided with the 3.0 update [source: X(ByteStix), verified 2026-07].
- [interpretation] This appears to be a case where adding Steam as a new distribution channel and pairing it with a major content update offset the natural decline on the original platform (its own launcher).

## Implications for Our Project
[interpretation] Declining users in a live-service game may be difficult to retain with new characters alone, and expanding distribution channels (entering a new platform) can itself be a separate rebound lever - however, this card's evidence cannot separate what percentage of the rebound came from the channel effect versus the content effect.
